Solar EPC & PV Mounting Structures

Most rooftop solar failures in Pakistan are structural, not electrical. We survey the roof, calculate the wind and seismic loads for your location, and design a hot-dip galvanised structure that suits the actual roof profile — shell, truss, RCC or sheet.

  • Structural survey and load assessment
  • Custom purlin, rafter and ballast design
  • Hot-dip galvanised fabrication
  • Rooftop, shell-roof and ground-mount systems
  • Module mounting and DC cable routing
  • Inverter platforms and equipment sheds

Electrical Engineering & Power Solutions

Fieldman Solution is a specialized electrical engineering and power solutions company providing design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance services for electrical systems across industrial, commercial, infrastructure, and energy projects.

Our expertise covers complete electrical power distribution systems from High Voltage (HV), Medium Voltage (MV), and Low Voltage (LV) applications. We provide reliable and customized solutions for industries including oil & gas, petroleum refineries, power plants, cement, sugar, textile, chemical, manufacturing, and infrastructure projects.

  • HV, MV & LV Switchgear Systems
  • HT/LT Panels, MCCs & PCCs
  • Electrical Distribution & Power Management Systems
  • Power & Distribution Transformers
  • AC & DC Motors and Motor Control Systems
  • Industrial Generators & Generator Synchronization Systems
  • Automatic Transfer Switches (ATS) & AMF Panels
  • Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs) & Soft Starters
  • Power Factor Correction & APFC Systems
  • Electrical Cabling, Cable Trays & Busbar Systems
  • Earthing, Lightning Protection & Electrical Safety Systems

  • Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Power Systems
  • Hybrid & Grid-Connected Solar Solutions
  • Solar Water Pumping Systems
  • Wind Energy & Renewable Power Solutions
  • Battery Energy Storage & Energy Management Solutions
  • Energy Efficiency & Power Quality Solutions

Fieldman Solution provides end-to-end electrical engineering services, from system design and load assessment to equipment selection, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning, and after-sales support. Our team works with clients to develop solutions that improve power reliability, operational efficiency, safety, and energy performance.

With expertise in both conventional and renewable energy systems, Fieldman Solution is capable of delivering integrated power solutions ranging from industrial electrical distribution and backup generation to solar, wind, and hybrid renewable energy systems.

Our commitment is to provide safe, efficient, technically compliant, and cost-effective electrical solutions while maintaining high standards of engineering quality, project execution, and customer satisfaction.

Instrumentation, Automation & Control Systems

Fieldman Solution provides comprehensive Instrumentation, Automation, and Process Control solutions for industrial and infrastructure projects. Our services cover the complete lifecycle of instrumentation systems, including engineering, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, calibration, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

  • Analytical Instruments & Process Analyzers
  • PLC-Based Control Systems
  • Distributed Control Systems (DCS)
  • SCADA & HMI Systems
  • Industrial Automation & Process Control
  • Remote I/O & Communication Systems
  • Motor Control & Automation Interfaces
  • Instrument Control Panels & Marshalling Cabinets
  • Instrument Cable Installation & Termination
  • Junction Boxes, Cable Trays & Instrument Tubing
  • Loop Checking, Function Testing & Commissioning
  • Instrument Calibration & Calibration Management
  • Pressure, Temperature, Flow & Level Calibration
  • Control Valve Testing & Calibration
  • Troubleshooting & Preventive Maintenance
  • PLC/DCS Modification, Programming & Integration
  • Plant Automation Upgrades and Modernization

Our experienced instrumentation engineers and technicians provide accurate calibration, reliable measurement, and effective process control solutions to improve plant safety, productivity, operational reliability, and process efficiency.

Fieldman Solution can execute instrumentation projects on a turnkey or work-package basis, covering engineering and instrument selection, procurement, installation, calibration, configuration, programming, loop checking, commissioning, and maintenance.

How We Work

From first site visit to documented handover

01

Survey & Consultation

We visit your site, record as-built conditions and understand the production constraints we have to work around.

02

Engineering & Proposal

Structural calculations, GA drawings and a detailed BOQ — issued with a firm price and a dated programme.

03

Mobilisation & Execution

Method statements, job safety analyses, materials and crews on site — with daily progress and HSE reporting.

04

Testing & Handover

Inspection, load and hydro testing, punch-list closure, as-built drawings and a documented handover pack.

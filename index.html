Who We Are

A contractor built around what actually happens on site

FieldMan was founded by engineers who spent their careers on plant floors, rooftops and shutdown windows — not behind desks. That is why our proposals account for access, sequencing and live production from the first drawing, and why our crews finish what the programme says they will finish.

We work as a single-source contractor across five disciplines, which removes the coordination gaps that cost industrial clients the most time: the handover between the civil team and the mechanical team, between the structure and the cabling, between fabrication and erection.

  • Turnkey delivery across civil, solar, electrical, mechanical and piping
  • In-house design, fabrication and erection capability
  • Work executed safely inside live, operating plants
  • Documented QA/QC and as-built handover on every project

How We Work

A process that protects your production

Industrial work is won or lost on sequencing. Here is how a FieldMan project runs from first call to final handover.

01

Survey & Consultation

We visit your site, record as-built conditions and understand the production constraints we have to work around.

02

Engineering & Proposal

Structural calculations, GA drawings and a detailed BOQ — issued with a firm price and a dated programme.

03

Mobilisation & Execution

Method statements, job safety analyses, materials and crews on site — with daily progress and HSE reporting.

04

Testing & Handover

Inspection, load and hydro testing, punch-list closure, as-built drawings and a documented handover pack.

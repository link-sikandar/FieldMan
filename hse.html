Health, Safety & Environment

Everyone who arrives on a FieldMan site goes home in the same condition. That is the standard the rest of the programme is built around.

Our Commitment

Safety is a condition of work, not a department

Industrial construction carries real hazards: work at height, hot work beside flammable materials, energised equipment, confined spaces and heavy lifts — often all in a plant that is still producing. FieldMan treats the control of those hazards as part of the engineering, not as paperwork produced afterwards.

Every scope we price includes the access, isolation, barricading and supervision needed to do it safely. If a job cannot be done safely in the window offered, we say so before we sign, rather than discovering it at two in the morning during a shutdown.

Our standing rule: any worker, from any trade, may stop a task they believe is unsafe — without needing permission and without consequence.

On Every Site

How we actually control risk

These are not aspirations — they are the controls your site supervisor will see applied from day one of mobilisation.

Job Safety Analysis

Every task is broken down and assessed before work starts. The JSA is signed by the crew performing it, not filed by an office.

Permit to Work

Hot work, confined space, work at height, excavation and electrical isolation each run under a permit issued and closed by an authorised person.

Toolbox Talks

Short daily briefings covering the day's hazards, the method, and the emergency arrangements for that specific work front.

PPE & Equipment

Helmets, safety footwear, eye and hearing protection, harnesses and flame-retardant clothing supplied and enforced — including for visitors.

Scaffolding & Access

Erected by trained scaffolders, tagged, and inspected before each shift. No improvised access platforms, ever.

Lifting Plans

Every crane lift runs to a written plan: load calculation, ground bearing, exclusion zone, rigging inspection and a nominated signaller.

Training & Competency

Welders, riggers, electricians and scaffolders work to documented qualifications, with records available for client audit.

Environmental Control

Waste segregation, spill kits at fuel and oil points, dust and noise suppression, and removal of all construction debris at handover.

Quality Assurance

Documented quality, not assumed quality

Safety and quality come from the same discipline: doing the work to a written method and recording that you did. Our QA/QC process runs alongside HSE on every project.

  • Approved drawings and method statements before work begins
  • Material test certificates retained and handed over
  • Welder qualification records and weld visual inspection
  • Dimensional checks and alignment records for erected steel
  • Hydro-testing, megger and earth resistance test reports
  • Punch list closed and signed before demobilisation
  • As-built drawings issued as part of the handover pack

Fieldman Solution HSE Policy

Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE) Policy — the full policy is set out below. A signed copy is available on request as part of vendor pre-qualification.

1. Policy Statement

Fieldman Solution is committed to conducting all engineering, procurement, fabrication, construction, installation, commissioning, maintenance, and project management activities in a manner that protects the health and safety of our employees, contractors, clients, visitors, and the public, while minimizing our impact on the environment.

We recognize that Health, Safety and Environmental protection are fundamental responsibilities and integral parts of our business operations. No project, schedule, or commercial objective shall take priority over the safety of people and protection of the environment.

Fieldman Solution is committed to achieving a workplace where zero accidents, injuries, occupational illnesses, environmental incidents, and unsafe practices are actively pursued through effective planning, risk management, training, supervision, and continuous improvement.

Fieldman Solution shall:

  • Comply with applicable national and local HSE laws, regulations, permits, and client requirements.
  • Establish and maintain safe working conditions at all project sites, workshops, offices, and operational areas.
  • Identify workplace hazards and assess associated risks before and during execution of work.
  • Implement appropriate controls to eliminate hazards or reduce risks to an acceptable level.
  • Provide appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and ensure its correct use.
  • Provide HSE training, toolbox talks, inductions, and awareness programs for employees and subcontractors.
  • Ensure that employees have the right and responsibility to stop unsafe work without fear of disciplinary action.
  • Maintain effective emergency preparedness and response procedures.
  • Investigate accidents, near misses, unsafe acts, and environmental incidents to identify root causes and prevent recurrence.
  • Maintain good housekeeping and safe access/egress at all workplaces.
  • Protect employees and workers from occupational health hazards through appropriate monitoring and preventive measures.
  • Minimize waste generation and promote responsible waste handling, segregation, recycling, and disposal.
  • Prevent unnecessary pollution of air, water, soil, and surrounding areas.
  • Promote efficient use of energy, water, fuel, raw materials, and other resources.
  • Select equipment, materials, and working methods with due consideration to health, safety, and environmental impacts.
  • Communicate HSE requirements clearly to employees, contractors, suppliers, visitors, and other relevant stakeholders.
  • Regularly monitor and review HSE performance and implement corrective and preventive actions.

Fieldman Solution is committed to protecting the physical and occupational health of all personnel involved in our activities. Our safety practices include:

  • Site and job-specific Risk Assessments and Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
  • Permit-to-Work systems where required.
  • Safe systems of work for high-risk activities.
  • Proper PPE selection and enforcement.
  • Electrical safety and isolation procedures.
  • Working-at-height controls.
  • Lifting and rigging safety.
  • Hot work and welding safety.
  • Confined-space entry controls.
  • Excavation and trenching safety.
  • Chemical and hazardous-material handling.
  • Machinery and equipment safety.
  • Fire prevention and emergency response.
  • First-aid facilities and emergency medical arrangements.
  • Safe transportation and material handling practices.

Fieldman Solution recognizes its responsibility to protect the environment throughout the project lifecycle. We shall strive to:

  • Prevent uncontrolled release of pollutants.
  • Properly segregate and dispose of industrial and general waste.
  • Control oil, chemical, and fuel spills.
  • Minimize dust, fumes, noise, and other environmental impacts.
  • Prevent contamination of soil and water resources.
  • Encourage recycling and reuse of materials wherever practicable.
  • Reduce unnecessary energy and water consumption.
  • Promote environmentally responsible procurement and construction practices.
  • Comply with applicable environmental requirements and project-specific environmental management plans.

Fieldman Solution considers competent personnel essential to safe project execution. Accordingly, we will provide appropriate:

  • HSE induction training.
  • Job-specific safety training.
  • Toolbox talks.
  • Emergency response training.
  • Fire safety training.
  • First-aid awareness.
  • PPE training.
  • Lifting and rigging awareness.
  • Electrical and instrumentation safety training.
  • Work-at-height and confined-space awareness.
  • Environmental awareness training.

Personnel shall only be assigned tasks for which they are appropriately trained, competent, and authorized.

All contractors and subcontractors working under Fieldman Solution shall be required to comply with applicable HSE requirements. Their performance shall be monitored through:

  • HSE induction and orientation.
  • Risk assessments and method statements.
  • Inspection and supervision.
  • PPE compliance.
  • Toolbox meetings.
  • Incident and near-miss reporting.
  • Corrective action tracking.
  • Periodic HSE performance reviews.

All accidents, injuries, near misses, property damage incidents, environmental incidents, and unsafe conditions shall be reported promptly.

Fieldman Solution will investigate significant incidents to determine root causes and contributing factors and implement corrective and preventive actions to reduce the possibility of recurrence.

Fieldman Solution shall maintain appropriate emergency preparedness arrangements based on the nature and risk profile of each project. Emergency planning may include response procedures for:

  • Fire and explosion.
  • Electrical emergencies.
  • Medical emergencies.
  • Chemical or fuel spills.
  • Gas releases.
  • Equipment failure.
  • Confined-space emergencies.
  • Severe weather and natural hazards.
  • Evacuation and rescue situations.

Emergency procedures shall be communicated to relevant personnel and periodically reviewed and tested where appropriate.

Fieldman Solution is committed to continually improving its HSE performance through:

  • Regular workplace inspections.
  • HSE audits.
  • Risk assessments.
  • Safety observations.
  • Incident and near-miss analysis.
  • Corrective and preventive actions.
  • Management reviews.
  • Employee participation and feedback.
  • Monitoring of HSE performance indicators.

Our goal is to develop a strong safety culture in which every individual takes personal responsibility for their own safety and the safety of others.

Management of Fieldman Solution shall provide the necessary leadership, resources, personnel, training, equipment, and supervision required to implement this policy effectively.

Every employee, contractor, and project team member is responsible for complying with HSE requirements and contributing to a safe and environmentally responsible workplace.

This HSE Policy shall be communicated to all relevant employees and stakeholders and shall be periodically reviewed and updated to ensure its continuing suitability, effectiveness, and alignment with applicable legal, client, and project requirements.

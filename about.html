Company Overview

Built on site experience, not on brochures

FieldMan is a multidiscipline engineering and construction contractor serving Pakistan’s manufacturing and energy sectors. We design, fabricate, erect, test and hand over — covering civil works, solar mounting systems, electrical and instrumentation, mechanical erection and piping systems.

The company was formed around a simple observation: industrial clients rarely fail because a contractor cannot weld or pour concrete. They fail because scopes are split between four contractors who do not talk to each other, because nobody surveyed the roof before fabricating the structure, or because the plant was never scheduled to stop when the work required it to.

FieldMan takes the whole scope, plans it against your production calendar, and carries the responsibility through to commissioning. Our engineers are on site — not only in the office — and our supervisors have the authority to solve problems the day they appear.

  • Registered engineering contractor with in-house design capability
  • Own fabrication facility and erection equipment
  • Qualified welders, riggers, electricians and supervisors
  • Documented HSE and QA/QC systems applied on every job

Why Choose Us

Six reasons clients keep calling us back

Single-Point Responsibility

One contract and one accountable team across civil, mechanical, electrical and solar scopes — no gaps between trades to manage.

In-House Engineering

Our own design desk produces structural calculations, GA drawings and BOQs, so site decisions are made quickly and correctly.

Safety Without Compromise

Documented JSAs, toolbox talks, inspected scaffolding and full PPE on every site. HSE is a condition of work, never an add-on.

Own Fabrication Facility

Shop fabrication under controlled conditions shortens site time, improves weld quality and keeps your plant running.

Certified Welders & Technicians

Qualified to recognised welding procedures with WPS/PQR documentation and third-party testing available on request.

Delivered On Schedule

Baseline programmes, weekly progress reporting and resource loading that holds up inside tight shutdown windows.

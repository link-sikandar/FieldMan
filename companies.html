Our Companies

The Fieldman Group is made up of three companies working under one accountable name — engineering and construction, technology, and Agro work.

Company 01

Fieldman Solution

Fieldman Solution is the Fieldman Group’s founding engineering and construction company, and the company behind the work shown across the rest of this site. It designs, fabricates, erects, tests and hands over industrial engineering scopes for clients in Pakistan’s manufacturing and energy sectors.

Fieldman Solution takes on civil and structural works, solar EPC and PV mounting structures, electrical and instrumentation, mechanical erection and fabrication, and piping systems — either as a full turnkey scope or as a single discipline alongside other contractors already on site.

  • Civil & Structural Works
  • Solar EPC & PV Structures
  • Electrical & Instrumentation
  • Mechanical Erection & Fabrication
  • Piping & Pipeline Systems
  • Maintenance & Plant Shutdowns

Company 02

Fieldman Tech

The Fieldman Group’s technology company, set up to carry the group’s IT and software work.

Fieldman Tech supports clients with the technology side of their operations — from day-to-day IT infrastructure and network support to custom software, websites and digital tools that help a business run more efficiently. It works alongside Fieldman Solution on the same projects where a client needs both engineering and technology delivered by one accountable group, and independently for clients who only need the technology side.

IT Infrastructure & Networking

Software & Web Development

Digital Transformation Consulting

Technical Support & Maintenance

Company 03

Agro Field

The Fieldman Group’s Agro company.

Agro Field carries the Fieldman Group’s Agro-focused work, supporting clients with Agro-related equipment, infrastructure and site support. As with the rest of the group, the aim is the same: one accountable team, engineering work delivered properly, and a company clients can call directly rather than chase through a chain of subcontractors.

  • Agricultural equipment & infrastructure support
  • Agro-industrial engineering support
  • Field & site support services
  • Supply and logistics coordination

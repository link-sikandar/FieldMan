Featured Project 01 — EPC

100 KLPD Distillery Unit — Pak Ethanol

Project Scope: Complete EPC Solution

Fieldman Solution successfully provided a complete Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) solution for a 100 KLPD (Kilo Litres Per Day) Distillery Unit for Pak Ethanol.

The project involved comprehensive engineering and execution support covering mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, piping, structural fabrication, equipment installation, and plant automation. Our multidisciplinary team coordinated the complete project activities to ensure smooth integration of various plant systems and timely execution.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Complete EPC Project Management
  • Mechanical Equipment Supply & Installation
  • Process & Utility Piping Fabrication and Erection
  • Storage Tank & Structural Fabrication
  • Mechanical Equipment Installation and Alignment
  • Electrical Power Distribution Systems
  • HV/MV/LV Electrical Systems
  • Motors, MCCs and Control Panels
  • Field Instrumentation
  • PLC/DCS & Process Automation
  • Instrument Installation, Calibration & Loop Checking
  • Testing & Commissioning
  • Plant Integration and Start-up Support

The project demonstrates Fieldman Solution’s capability to execute large-scale process industry projects through an integrated EPC approach, utilizing its multidisciplinary expertise in Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Automation, and Project Management.

Featured Project 02 — EPC

65 m³/h Biogas Plant — Ethanol Plant

Project Scope: Complete EPC Solution

Fieldman Solution successfully executed a complete Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) solution for a 65 m³/h Biogas Plant utilizing waste/effluent generated from the outlet of an ethanol plant.

The project involved the integration of the biogas generation system with the existing ethanol plant, including process engineering, mechanical systems, piping, electrical power distribution, instrumentation, automation, and complete plant installation and commissioning.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Complete EPC Project Management
  • Biogas Process System Engineering
  • Feed/Effluent Handling & Transfer Systems
  • Biogas Digester & Associated Equipment
  • Mechanical Equipment Supply & Installation
  • Process & Utility Piping Fabrication and Erection
  • Storage Tanks and Structural Fabrication
  • Pumps, Blowers and Auxiliary Equipment
  • Electrical Power Distribution & MCC Systems
  • Motors and Electrical Control Systems
  • Field Instrumentation
  • PLC/SCADA-Based Process Automation
  • Instrument Installation, Calibration & Loop Checking
  • Electrical & Instrumentation Cabling
  • Testing, Commissioning & Start-up Support
  • Integration with Existing Ethanol Plant Utilities

The project reflects Fieldman Solution’s capability to deliver integrated waste-to-energy and renewable energy solutions for process industries, combining expertise in Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Automation, and EPC Project Management to achieve reliable and efficient plant operation.

Featured Project 03 — EPC

Waste Heat Recovery Boiler — Dadu Energy, Dadu

Project Scope: Complete EPC Solution

Fieldman Solution successfully executed a complete Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) solution for a Waste Heat Recovery Boiler (WHRB) project at Dadu Energy, Dadu.

The project involved the utilization of waste heat from the existing process/generation system to produce useful steam, improving overall plant energy efficiency and reducing thermal energy losses. Fieldman Solution provided multidisciplinary engineering and execution services covering mechanical, piping, structural, electrical, instrumentation, automation, installation, testing, and commissioning.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Steam & Condensate Piping Systems
  • Pumps, Valves and Mechanical Auxiliaries
  • Electrical Power Distribution & MCC Systems
  • Motors and Electrical Control Systems
  • Field Instrumentation & Process Measurement
  • PLC/DCS-Based Automation & Control
  • Instrument Installation, Calibration & Loop Checking
  • Electrical & Instrumentation Cabling
  • Testing, Pre-Commissioning & Commissioning
  • Plant Integration and Start-up Support

This project demonstrates Fieldman Solution’s capability to deliver integrated energy-efficiency and process engineering projects, combining expertise in Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation, Automation, Piping, Fabrication, and EPC Project Management for industrial and power-sector applications.

Featured Project 04 — Solar EPC

900 kW Solar Power System — National Foods, Korangi

Project Scope: Complete EPC Solution

Fieldman Solution successfully executed a 900 kW Solar Power System for National Foods at Korangi, Karachi, providing an integrated renewable energy solution to support the facility’s electrical power requirements.

The project was delivered on a complete Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) basis, covering system engineering, equipment procurement, installation, electrical integration, testing, commissioning, and operational support.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Complete Solar PV System Engineering & EPC
  • Solar PV Module Supply & Installation
  • Grid-Tied Solar Inverter System
  • Solar Mounting Structure Design & Installation
  • DC & AC Cabling Systems
  • DC Combiner Boxes and Protection Systems
  • AC Distribution & Protection Panels
  • LT Electrical Integration
  • Earthing & Lightning Protection System
  • Solar Monitoring & Performance Monitoring System
  • Electrical Protection and Safety Systems
  • Testing, Commissioning & Performance Verification
  • Grid Synchronization & Plant Integration
  • Operation & Maintenance Support

The project demonstrates Fieldman Solution’s capability to deliver large-scale commercial and industrial solar power projects, providing reliable and energy-efficient renewable energy solutions through its expertise in Electrical Engineering, Renewable Energy, Project Management, Installation, Testing, and Commissioning.

Featured Project 05 — Material Supply

Alloy & Specialized Material Supply

Project Scope: Material Sourcing & Supply

Fieldman Solution sourced and supplied alloy and specialized piping materials for a client’s process piping requirements, managing procurement, quality inspection and export packing through to delivery.

Our procurement team coordinated with certified mills and fabricators to secure ASTM-grade alloy steel pipe and fittings, with each batch inspected and documented before being packed for shipment.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Alloy Steel Pipe & Fitting Procurement
  • ASTM-Grade Material Sourcing
  • Mill & Fabricator Coordination
  • Incoming Material Quality Inspection
  • Documentation & Material Traceability
  • Export Packing & Logistics Coordination
  • Delivery to Site

This project reflects Fieldman Solution’s capability to support clients beyond site execution — sourcing and quality-checking specialized materials so the right grade of steel arrives on site, on schedule, every time.

Featured Project 06 — Shutdown Maintenance & Installation

Flare Tip Maintenance & Cooling Tower Installation — EPRF

Project Scope: Shutdown Maintenance & Installation

Fieldman Solution carried out a flare tip maintenance job during a planned shutdown at EPRF, alongside the installation of a new cooling tower complete with a cast concrete basin.

The two work-fronts were coordinated within the same site mobilisation — flare tip inspection and repair during the shutdown window, and cooling tower erection with its supporting concrete basin executed in parallel.

Major Scope of Work:

  • Flare Tip Shutdown Maintenance
  • Flare Tip Inspection & Repair
  • Shutdown Planning & Site Coordination
  • Cooling Tower Installation
  • Concrete Basin Construction
  • Mechanical Erection & Alignment
  • Site Safety & HSE Supervision

The project demonstrates Fieldman Solution’s ability to run multiple work-fronts — shutdown maintenance and new equipment installation — safely and on schedule within a single site mobilisation.
